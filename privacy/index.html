How we handle your information

Planéir is operated by Gerry Boylan in Ireland. Contact hello@planeir.ie.

Local form preview

This notice describes the workspace service. In the current preview, submissions are disabled. Your draft is saved in this browser; it is not sent to Planéir or an AI provider. Use Clear form to remove it.

What the workspace collects

Your name and email for return access, your question, the household details and figures you choose to enter, generated illustrations, and records of the terms and notices you acknowledge. Estimates and unknown figures are identified.

Do not enter PPS numbers, passwords, PINs, full account or card numbers, an exact address, identity documents or unnecessary information about someone else.

Why we use it

We use your information to prepare and save the workspace you request, provide return access and handle corrections or deletion. The lawful basis for this service is performance of our agreement with you under the Terms of Use.

Limited security and operational records support our legitimate interests in preventing misuse, controlling service costs and maintaining reliability. Optional calls are a separate service you request. The workspace does not publish your answers, sell your financial information or send it to an adviser.

Automatic processing and AI

AI helps identify the scenarios to model from your objectives and organise the relevant modules. Financial calculations run in Planéir’s calculation engines. These results are generated automatically. Gerry does not routinely review individual workspaces.

Relevant figures, objectives and written context are sent to the AI provider to identify scenarios and organise the relevant modules. This does not constitute a lending approval, product recommendation or decision that changes your financial arrangements. Read all the assumptions included with your results.

Who processes the information

  • Cloudflare provides the application infrastructure and encrypted storage.
  • OpenAI processes information for AI assistance.
  • Resend provides service emails and return-access emails when connected.
  • GitHub hosts the website pages.
  • Zoom hosts the optional private education calls.

Authorised support, including Gerry, may access information when needed to operate the service, investigate a problem or respond to your request. A private workspace does not mean that no service provider or authorised support can access it.

Providers may process information outside the European Economic Area. The exact providers, processing locations and applicable transfer safeguards must be confirmed before workspace submissions are enabled; no claim about model training or zero provider retention is made here.

Saved access and deletion

Your workspace remains until you delete it. Deleting it removes the stored household information, conversations and results associated with it. In the local form preview, use Clear form to remove the draft from this device.

Separately retained security, email-delivery or booking records must be identified with their own purpose and retention period before the relevant information is collected. A workspace deletion does not silently change the records of a separate service you requested.

Optional private call

After viewing your analysis, you can request a call with Gerry. The call details appear beside your results. Requesting a call shares your contact details and the discussion note you enter with Gerry. Your profile and calculation results are not copied automatically; you can share the analysis on screen during the call. Gerry proposes a time and emails a Zoom invitation to accept or decline. Calls are private and unrecorded by default. No adviser introduction is included.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interests, where applicable. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. Email hello@planeir.ie. You may complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission.

Existing applications and invited sessions

Earlier case-video applications remain covered by the original application notice. The separately invited consumer beta remains covered by its original consumer notice, including its seven-day session expiry and optional voice processing. Those permissions are not permission to create a new workspace.
