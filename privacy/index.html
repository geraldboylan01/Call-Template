How we handle your information

Planeir is operated by Gerry Boylan in Ireland. Contact hello@planeir.ie.

Demo requests and saved information

Currently available for demos only. Requesting access sends your information to Planeir for storage and demo access management. Analysis generation is not connected yet; this form sends no information to an AI provider. Unfinished edits are saved in your browser until you submit them.

What the workspace collects

Your name and email for private return access, the goals, household details and figures you enter, your demo access status, confirmed information, and records of the terms and notices you acknowledge. Estimates and unknown figures are identified.

Do not enter PPS numbers, passwords, PINs, full account or card numbers, an exact address, identity documents or unnecessary information about someone else.

Why we use it

We use your information to save your demo request, manage approval or an invitation, provide private return access, and handle corrections, confirmation or deletion. The lawful basis for this service is performance of our agreement with you under the Terms of Use.

Limited security and operational records support our legitimate interests in preventing misuse, controlling service costs and maintaining reliability. Optional calls are a separate service you request. The workspace does not publish your answers, sell your financial information or send it to an adviser.

Analysis and AI

Analysis generation is not connected to this demo flow. Requesting access, editing your information and choosing Generate my analysis save information and confirmation only. They do not start calculations or send your information to an AI provider. Any future analysis processing will be explained before it is enabled.

Who processes the information

  • Cloudflare runs the API and database. Demo contact details, goals, figures and confirmations are encrypted in storage.
  • Resend delivers demo receipts, private return links and access emails. Financial figures are not included in these emails.
  • GitHub hosts the website pages.
  • Zoom hosts separately requested private education calls; it is not used to submit demo information.

Authorised support, including Gerry, may access information when needed to operate the service, investigate a problem or respond to your request. A private workspace does not mean that no service provider or authorised support can access it.

Providers may process information outside the European Economic Area. Providers operate under their service and data-processing terms, including applicable international transfer safeguards. Contact us for information about these arrangements. Demo submissions do not use AI processing.

Saved access and deletion

Your saved demo information remains until you delete it. Use Delete my saved information in your private form to remove your contact details, figures, confirmation, access links, sessions and associated email queue records from the active service. Use Clear this device to remove the browser draft and end this browser’s private session without deleting your saved request. Provider backups follow their own recovery lifecycle.

Private email links expire after 24 hours; browser sessions expire after seven days. Expired access records are cleaned up by scheduled maintenance. Email delivery metadata is kept for up to 30 days, with encrypted email bodies removed after sending or final failure. Rate-limit counters expire logically after one hour and are removed by routine cleanup. Email providers keep their own delivery records under their terms. Separate booking records are governed by the service you requested.

Optional private call

After viewing your analysis, you can request a call with Gerry. The call details appear beside your results. Requesting a call shares your contact details and the discussion note you enter with Gerry. Your profile and calculation results are not copied automatically; you can share the analysis on screen during the call. Gerry proposes a time and emails a Zoom invitation to accept or decline. Calls are private and unrecorded by default. No adviser introduction is included.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interests, where applicable. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. Email hello@planeir.ie. You may complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission.
