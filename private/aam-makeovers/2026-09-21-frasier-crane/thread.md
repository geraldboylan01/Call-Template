## #7 Brendan Burgess (Founder), 17 Aug 2026

> Quoting the child's age, the cash and company shares, and the daughter paragraph.

Normally, the advice to clear the mortgage would be correct. I don't think it is in this case.

Your priority should be to help your daughter get on the housing ladder. You have €370k in liquid assets. You reduce this to €280k if you pay off your mortgage.

What is your daughter's income? What is her likely income? Will she get a mortgage? How much would a property cost?

I would suggest that you give her a mortgage to buy a home for herself. That is a full legally binding mortgage. And she must repay it. The rate can be favourable.

If she wants to buy something more expensive, it becomes a problem. The bank won't give her a mortgage unless you give her a gift and I don't think you should gift her that amount. This would be particularly important if she buys the property jointly with a partner.

I am not suggesting that she has to buy a property now. But you should be talking to her now and planning for her to get on the property market when she is ready. You could make it conditional on her saving up €50k.

## #11 Brendan Burgess, 18 Aug 2026

I recommend to people who want to buy an apartment for their child, to give them a mortgage instead. It makes the child more responsible. It protects the parents if their financial circumstances decline. And if the child gets into a relationship which subsequently breaks down, then, the parents' interest is protected. (I have seen such an informal loan to a couple claimed as a gift when the relationship subsequently broke down.)

It makes no sense to rent if you can buy. Even for 5 years.

If she gets a mortgage, you won't be able to give her a mortgage as well. It would have to be documented as a gift.
