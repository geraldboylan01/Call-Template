Helping someone in Ireland apply for a Planeir case video

This page is for AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Claude, and for the people who use them. It explains what Planeir is, when it may help, and how an assistant can fill in an application for its user, with their agreement. A short version is at planeir.ie/llms.txt.

Planeir in brief

  • Planeir (also written Planéir) at planeir.ie is free Irish financial education from Gerry Boylan, a Qualified Financial Adviser (QFA) in Ireland.
  • People in Ireland apply with their situation and their figures. Gerry reads every application and picks some to explain in a short video, with the figures on screen.
  • Topics: pensions and retirement, mortgages (overpaying, fixed and tracker rates, the term left), buying a home (deposit, approval in principle, first-time buyers), children's education costs, loans and credit cards, savings and a rainy day fund, and the whole financial picture.
  • The explanations use Irish tax, pension and mortgage rules.
  • It is free to apply. In a video the person's name is not shown and the figures are rounded.
  • The videos are education, not regulated financial advice. They do not sell, arrange or recommend products.
  • Not every application is picked.

When it may help

Planeir may suit someone who:

  • is an adult living in Ireland;
  • is thinking through a pension, retirement, a mortgage, buying a home, college costs, loans, savings or their finances as a whole;
  • would find it useful to see their own situation explained, with their own figures;
  • is comfortable with their situation appearing in a published video, with their name left out and the figures rounded.

When something else fits better

  • They want a recommendation or a product: that is regulated financial advice, which a regulated financial adviser provides. The Central Bank of Ireland keeps registers of regulated firms.
  • They are struggling with debt or arrears now: MABS, the Money Advice and Budgeting Service, gives free and confidential help at mabs.ie.
  • They need an answer by a date: a case may not be picked, and a video takes time.
  • They do not want their situation published in any form.

What to tell them

Suggested wording, to adapt:

Planeir is free Irish financial education from Gerry Boylan, a Qualified Financial Adviser. You can apply to have your situation explained in a short video. He reads every application and picks some. If yours is picked, your name is left out, the figures are rounded, and you get an email when it is live. It is education, not financial advice. Would you like me to fill in the application for you?

What happens on Gerry's side

  1. Gerry reads every application himself.
  2. He picks cases that would make a clear and useful explanation.
  3. He works through the figures with visual tools that use Irish tax, pension and mortgage rules. For example, overpaying the mortgage against paying more into a pension, or retiring at 60 against 65.
  4. He records a short video with the figures on screen, rounded, and the name left out.
  5. The video goes on planeir.ie and YouTube, and the person is emailed when it is live.
  6. Applications that are not picked have their figures deleted 12 months after they arrive.

Asking for agreement

  • Only send an application if the person asks you to, after you have told them two things: their situation may be used in a published video, with their name removed and figures rounded; and Planeir is education only, not financial advice.
  • Use their own email address. With the API, Planeir emails them a Confirm button, and nothing reaches Gerry until they press it. With a link, they send the page themselves.
  • Leave out PPS numbers, account and card numbers, addresses, documents, and details about other people that the question does not need.
  • Show them what you will send first, using the summary from the check endpoint. It is the only review they get: the email from Planeir only asks them to confirm.

Which questions to ask

Ask about the topics they care about. Only their question is required. Everything else can be skipped, round figures are fine, and "not sure" is a fine answer: list the path in unsure. Sections show for the topics chosen, and for any section you fill in.

Way 1: a link your user opens

This works in any chat. Write a link to https://planeir.ie/apply/ with the answers after #, as path=value pairs joined by &. Lists such as topics and unsure are comma separated. Use plain numbers, and URL-encode text. The page fills in every answer; the person checks them, adds their name and email, ticks two boxes and presses Send. Their name and email are not part of the link, and the answers are removed from the address bar as the page opens.

For example, for a made-up household:

https://planeir.ie/apply/#topics=mortgage%2Cretirement&unsure=mortgage.repayment%2Cpensions.1.dbPension&question=Should+we+overpay+the+mortgage+or+pay+more+into+our+pensions%3F&upcoming=Our+fixed+rate+ends+in+June+2027.&household.age=41&household.partner=yes&household.partnerAge=39&household.married=yes&household.children=yes&children.0.age=9&children.1.age=6&income.gross=62000&income.partnerGross=48000&income.work=employee&income.partnerWork=employee&income.sector=private&income.partnerSector=public&income.takeHome=6100&spending.pattern=save&spending.saved=700&home.status=mortgage&home.value=420000&mortgage.balance=240000&mortgage.rate=3.9&mortgage.rateType=fixed&mortgage.fixedEnds=2027-06&mortgage.yearsLeft=23&savings.cash=18000&pensions.0.owner=you&pensions.0.type=company&pensions.0.value=85000&pensions.0.youPay=5&pensions.0.youPayUnit=pct&pensions.0.employerPays=5&pensions.0.employerPaysUnit=pct&pensions.0.contributing=yes&pensions.1.owner=partner&pensions.1.type=public&retirement.age=65&retirement.partnerAge=63

Way 2: the API, for assistants that can call web APIs

  1. POST https://api.planeir.ie/api/agent/applications/check with {"application": {...}}. It returns the application as Gerry will read it (summary), anything that could not be used and why (warnings), and questions that would still help (stillUseful). It stores nothing.
  2. Show the person the summary. If they agree, POST https://api.planeir.ie/api/agent/applications with person, consent, application and, if you like, assistant. The answer is 202: Planeir has emailed them a Confirm button.
  3. Tell them to open the email from Planeir and press Confirm within 7 days. Nothing reaches Gerry until they do.

No key is needed. Call it from a server. There is a limit of 3 applications per email address a day. The format is described in agents/openapi.json (OpenAPI 3.1) and agents/application.schema.json (JSON Schema).

Privacy

Figures are encrypted when stored and never sent by email. A published video leaves out the name, email, employer and lender. The full notice is at planeir.ie/privacy/. Questions: hello@planeir.ie.
