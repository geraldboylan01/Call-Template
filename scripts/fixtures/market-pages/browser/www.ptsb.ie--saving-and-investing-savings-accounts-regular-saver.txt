Features and Benefits
Easy monthly savings: Choose any amount to save monthly, from €0 to €2,500 per month. You can save a set amount each month or just put some money away whenever you have a little extra.
Competitive rates: Earn a competitive variable interest rate on balances’ up to €75,000.00.
Easy access: You can access your money whenever you want. Just remember to give us 21 days' notice to avoid an early withdrawal charge.
Joint Account option: Open a maximum of two 21 Day Regular Saver Accounts either in your own name, or jointly with another or others.
Understanding the Account
Withdrawal options:
You can withdraw your money at any time, but remember to give us 21 days' notice to avoid an early withdrawal charge.
Notice can be applied through Open24.ie, Open24 Telephone Banking, the PTSB App and in branch
Once the notice period has elapsed, you have up to 30 calendar days' to make the withdrawal without charge.
Annual Equivalent Rate (AER): A preview of how much interest you could earn each year if you kept your money in the account for a full year and the interest rate didn't change.
Deposit Interest Retention Tax (DIRT): A tax payable on the interest you earn from your Savings or Deposit Account. It is automatically deducted when you receive your funds. However, you may not have to pay DIRT. Further information on DIRT exemptions can be located on the Revenue website.
Interest is paid on the first working day after 20 November each year and is subject to DIRT at the prevailing rate which is subject to change.

How to open an account

You can open an account in two ways:

I'm a PTSB customer: You can open an account in your sole name by calling our Deposits Team on 0818 50 24 24 or +353 1 212 4101. Lines are open 9am to 5pm Monday to Friday (excluding public holidays). Or by making an appointment to attend one of our branches.
I'm a new PTSB customer: You can open an account by making an appointment at one of our branches with our team.
