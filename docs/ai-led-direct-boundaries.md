# Current direct-module semantic boundary

Source `30eab9e`, inspected 10 September 2026. This accompanies the live recovery experiment, not a production implementation. It covers the intended `modulePlannerMode=apply` lane. Checked-in deployment flags are off; no claim is made about deployed configuration. Unused legacy interpretation is excluded, except the still-callable tool bridge below.

**A — keep execution/structural machinery. B — replace deterministic semantic interpretation with AI. C — explicit product/contract decision before changing.** A routine can contain more than one category. Passing tests establish behaviour, not architectural legitimacy.

## Tests to keep, reframe or retire

Keep the stale-certificate/correction regression: `check-first20-verifier-rejection.mjs` was rerun during this review and passed **3/3**. It proves fresh rejection retires the offer, the lowest executor rejects the old certificate, and no engine runs. Also keep delivery/causal binding, unknown-action chronology, tamper rejection, native maths, budget/cancel, billing and execution-idempotency invariants.

Change tests that require a particular semantic mechanism:

- `check-live-completion-matrix.mjs:201` requires approval to cause zero model calls. It encodes the regex bypass, not a necessary safety property.
- `check-live-execution-approval.mjs:28`, `check-live-typed-confirmation.mjs:127` and `check-live-certified-approval.mjs:221` pin phrase/dialect recognition. Keep those phrases as semantic eval cases; remove the requirement that a deterministic grammar interpret them.
- `check-consumer-live.mjs:699` and 2619 pin financial question dictionaries. Keep the no-repeat user outcome.
- `check-module-input-display.mjs` treats hidden-path coverage as success. It must not justify suppressing a valid AI clarification about a client-selectable mode or assumption.
- `check-first20-repair-preservation.mjs` pins narrow-patch scopes, call counts and preservation. These are honest properties of that implementation, not proof it is the best architecture. Keep the outcome that fresh rejection blocks execution; allow a fully reconsidered candidate to change meaning only with new independent approval and informed confirmation.
- Its assertions at 191, 263 and 330 retain the original proposal after rejected or unsupported repair. Keeping an archived candidate is fine; these assertions must not justify leaving that candidate as current financial knowledge after the AI correctly retracts a value. Add an explicit ready-to-collecting recovery outcome while preserving zero execution and certificate revocation.
- `check-live-internal-repair.mjs:281` pins 'repair fixes representation, never meaning'. Preserve the prohibition on inventing an answer to genuine uncertainty. Do not forbid AI from correcting its own mistaken interpretation of an already complete conversation.
- `check-first20-assumption-floor.mjs` protects disclosure visibility. Keep approved-value/disclosure outcomes, while reconsidering value-equality and citation-presence inference as the source-attribution mechanism.

The five new `ai-led-holdouts.mjs` fixtures are oracles for synthetic examples, not parsers in production. Their owner checks must not migrate into the runtime. Additional live execution detail is in [ai-led-live-execution.md](ai-led-live-execution.md).
