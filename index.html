How Planéir works

From your figures to a clearer picture.

1. Enter your figures

Tell Planéir about your goals, income, home, savings and pensions. Estimates are fine. Skip anything you don’t know.

2. Get your analysis

AI helps identify the scenarios relevant to your objectives and organise your modules. Planéir runs the calculations.

3. Explore the outcomes

Compare modelled outcomes, read all the assumptions and return to your saved workspace. Results are generated automatically.

Why I built Planéir

Financial understanding should not be a privilege.

I’m Gerry Boylan, a Qualified Financial Adviser. I’ve worked with clients across every part of the financial advice process. I built Planéir because the depth of financial understanding available to wealthy clients should be within everyone’s reach.

Technology lets us bring that depth to more people: a clear picture of where you stand, comparisons of the choices ahead, and the assumptions behind each outcome.

Questions

What to expect.

What is Planéir?

Planéir brings detailed financial education beyond the wealthy few. Designed by Gerry Boylan, who has worked with clients across every part of the financial advice process, it uses technology to help you understand your own figures and compare the effect of different choices.

Is the workspace free?

Yes. Creating and using your workspace is free.

Does Gerry review my result?

Results are generated automatically. Gerry designed the app but does not routinely review individual results. You can request an optional call after viewing your analysis.

How does AI help?

AI helps identify the scenarios to model from your objectives and organise the relevant modules. Financial calculations run in Planéir’s calculation engines. Read all the assumptions included with your results.

Is my workspace private?

Your workspace is not published. Service providers process information to run the app, and authorised support may access it when needed. The privacy notice explains this.

Can I return to my workspace?

Yes. Your workspace stays saved until you delete it.

What if I don’t know a figure?

Estimates are fine, and you can skip figures you do not know. Missing information may prevent a particular calculation. The app identifies what is needed.

Is this financial advice?

Planéir provides financial education and modelled comparisons. It does not provide regulated advice or recommend financial products. Read all the assumptions and take appropriate professional advice before acting.

Can I talk through my results with Gerry?

Yes. After you receive your analysis, you can request an optional call with Gerry Boylan, the person who created Planéir. You’ll find the details beside your results.
