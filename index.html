How Planeir helps

Financial topics are easier to understand when the moving parts are visible.

Planeir is built for Irish people who want to understand the decision in front of them before they act. A free recorded education call can make pensions, mortgages, retirement, protection, and financial goals easier to follow in real time.

What people often need

A clearer picture of options, trade-offs, and whether they may be on track.

  • Plain-English context for Irish pensions, mortgages, and retirement questions
  • Visual explanations that make assumptions and trade-offs easier to follow
  • A better sense of what to learn, check, or discuss with an authorised professional
What Planeir does not do

It does not replace regulated personal advice or product recommendations.

The call is educational. It can help you understand the shape of a decision, but it does not tell you what product to buy, what policy to take out, or what action is suitable for your personal circumstances.

Education first. Advice only from an authorised professional.

Start with a straightforward conversation

You do not need to have everything figured out before you talk.

You can start with the question that is on your mind, whether it is retirement, housing, long-term security, or a decision you have been carrying around for a while. You do not need to explain everything perfectly, but a little background makes the conversation much more useful from the start.

Free recorded education calls

Calls are currently free for people willing to take part in a recorded educational conversation.

These sessions may be used in edited form as educational content online, including on platforms such as YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and similar channels. The goal is to create useful Irish financial education conversations that help both the participant and a wider audience.

You do not need to show your face. Camera off is absolutely fine, and you can use a first name or a different name if you prefer. The call remains educational only.
