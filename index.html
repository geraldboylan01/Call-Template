How it works

1. Send your situation

Fill in what you know about your income, home, savings and pensions. It takes about 10 minutes, and you can skip anything you don't know.

2. Gerry picks cases to explain

He reads every application and picks some to explain in a short video, using Irish tax, pension and mortgage rules.

3. Watch your video

If yours is picked, he emails you when the video is live. Your name is not shown and the figures are rounded.

What the videos are

Led by Gerry, built for Ireland

Gerry is a QFA qualified adviser. Each video works through a real person's figures, not a general script.

See the options, not just the maths

Visuals show choices side by side, like overpaying the mortgage or paying more into a pension, so the trade-offs make sense.

No selling in the videos

The videos explain options and trade-offs. They do not sell, arrange or recommend a product.
