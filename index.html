Why I built Planéir

Financial understanding should not be a privilege.

I’m Gerry Boylan, a Qualified Financial Adviser. I’ve worked with clients across every part of the financial advice process. I built Planéir because the depth of financial understanding available to wealthy clients should be within everyone’s reach.

Technology lets us bring that depth to more people: a clear picture of where you stand, comparisons of the choices ahead, and the assumptions behind each outcome.

Questions

What to expect.

What is Planéir?

Planéir helps you understand the different routes to your financial goals through modelled comparisons of your choices, figures and assumptions. It is built for Ireland and currently available for demos only.

How do I get demo access?

Share your goals and figures using the demo request form. We save your information and email you when access is available. If you already have a demo invitation, you can use it to continue directly.

What can I do in this demo?

You can save, review and confirm your information. Analysis generation is not connected yet. No analysis starts when you request access or confirm your figures.

How will AI help with the analysis?

AI helps identify the scenarios to model from your objectives and organise the relevant modules. Financial calculations run in Planéir’s calculation engines. AI processing is not connected to the demo request form.

Is my information private?

Your saved information is not published. Service providers process information to run the service, and authorised support may access it when needed. The privacy notice explains this.

Can I return to my information?

Yes. Use your private email link to reopen and update your saved form, including while waiting for approval. Your information stays saved until you delete it.

What if I don’t know a figure?

Estimates are fine. Skip anything you don’t know. You can return to update your information later.

Is this financial advice?

Planéir provides financial education and modelled comparisons. It does not provide regulated advice or recommend financial products. Read all the assumptions and take appropriate professional advice before acting.
