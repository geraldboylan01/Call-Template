Consumer disclosure

Clear by design

Your information, your choices.

This disclosure explains what happens when you use Planéir’s consumer planning journey. The journey is for financial education: it helps you organise information, review it, and run transparent illustrations. It does not provide regulated financial, mortgage, tax, legal, product, or approval advice.

A private browser session

Your access credential stays in this browser tab and is not used as an adviser login.

AI is optional

If offered and selected, AI can organise draft facts. It never calculates the results.

Sharing is a separate choice

Nothing is sent to Gerry unless you expressly consent after reviewing your results.

Your planning space

A separate, time-limited consumer session

When you begin, Planéir creates an opaque private credential. The browser keeps it in sessionStorage, which is scoped to this tab. It is not placed in a URL, adviser cookie, or browser localStorage. Closing the tab removes the browser’s copy, so the session may no longer be recoverable from that browser.

The session stores the information you choose to provide, conversation exchanges, reviewed profile revisions, and calculation results. Sensitive payloads are encrypted before they are stored by the service.

The server applies the session lifetime configured for the active journey. Your actual expiry is shown in the journey after the session is created; no fixed duration is assumed on this page.

Share only what is needed

Do not enter high-risk identifiers

Financial totals and approximate figures may be useful. Direct identity, security, and account credentials are not needed for this educational journey.

  • Do not enter PPS numbers, passwords, passcodes, PINs, or banking credentials.
  • Do not enter complete bank-account, card, or IBAN details.
  • Do not provide passport, driving-licence, or other identity-document details or copies.
  • Do not provide an exact home address or unnecessary information about another person.
The service applies validation and redaction safeguards, but those safeguards are not a reason to submit information the journey asks you to avoid.

Optional assistance

What AI may do—and what it cannot do

AI assistance is used only when the option is available and you choose it. It may interpret the words you type, propose draft profile details, and help select a clearer next question. AI can make mistakes, so proposed details remain drafts until you review and confirm them.

Where AI assistance is active, the in-journey control lets you withdraw that choice for future messages. Withdrawal applies to later messages; it does not undo processing already completed. The journey then continues with structured, rules-only questions.

If you opt in, Planéir sends your latest message, the current question, a bounded conversation summary, and the part of your planning profile relevant to that question to OpenAI through its Responses API. The request disables application storage with store: false. Any provider-side retention or abuse monitoring is governed by Planéir’s approved OpenAI account data controls and processing policy; AI stays disabled until that policy has been reviewed and configured.

AI is not allowed to calculate financial outputs, approve a mortgage or product, decide eligibility, or silently write trusted facts. Those boundaries remain enforced even when AI helps with the conversation.

Adviser-test option

Using your own Codex or ChatGPT subscription manually

During the adviser-only test cohort, Planéir may offer a manual prompt-copy helper. It creates a bounded prompt from the current Planéir question and the draft answer visible in your browser. Planéir copies that prompt to your clipboard only after you press the copy button; it does not send the prompt to OpenAI or connect to your OpenAI account.

If you choose to paste the prompt into your own Codex or ChatGPT workspace, that separate workspace’s privacy, retention, and subscription terms apply. Review the copied text first. Planéir invitation and private session credentials are automatically removed from the copied text. Nothing returns to Planéir unless you review the rewritten answer, paste it into the journey, and submit it.

A ChatGPT subscription cannot act as an API credential for the live Planéir service. This manual boundary keeps your subscription login out of Planéir while preserving the normal profile review, correction, and deterministic-calculation controls.

Code-owned outputs

Deterministic calculations

Financial illustrations are produced by tested, versioned calculation code using the profile revision you confirm. Results should show their assumptions, warnings, information gaps, and calculation provenance so you can understand what shaped the figure.

These outputs are educational scenarios rather than personalised regulated advice, lender decisions, guarantees, quotations, or product recommendations. Time-sensitive rules and assumptions should be checked before acting on an illustration.

Optional human help

A separate handoff to Gerry

After results are available, you may separately consent to send a reviewed package to Gerry Boylan. The journey tells you what will be shared before you consent: your name, email, optional phone number, the help you request, and a minimal consent receipt with a non-financial analysis/revision reference. Your detailed profile and calculation results are not automatically copied into the adviser pipeline.

This handoff is not automatic. It does not opt you into hidden marketing, an adviser marketplace, bidding, sponsored ranking, or product promotion. Your free results do not depend on agreeing to a handoff.

Once delivered, the handoff becomes a separate adviser record. Its retention period is set by the active handoff policy and may differ from the consumer-session lifetime. Planéir must present the applicable retention wording before handoff is enabled; this page deliberately does not hardcode a duration.

Before or after delivery, the journey’s Privacy controls let you withdraw the handoff and purge the encrypted bridge copy. If it has already reached Gerry, withdrawal cannot recall the separate adviser record; the control tells you to contact Planéir about that record.

Your control

Expiry, deletion, and an already-delivered handoff

You can use Delete my session in the journey to remove the consumer conversation, profile revisions, analyses, module runs, events, and private session access. Undelivered pending or failed handoff data is removed with the session. Sessions also expire under the lifetime configured for the active journey.

If you already expressly sent a handoff and delivery completed, deleting the consumer session does not recall that separate adviser record. Its active handoff policy applies. You can contact Planéir to ask what is held or request an appropriate correction or deletion, subject to any retention obligations that apply to the delivered record.
