What we’ll talk about

  1. Your goals for the future. Things like retirement, buying a home, or paying for education.
  2. Your finances. What you earn, what you spend, and what you own and owe.
  3. A starting point. Planéir uses your answers to build a personalised plan and shows every assumption it makes.

What to have ready

  • Your goals When you want to reach them and what they might cost
  • Income Your take-home salary, and a partner’s if you’re planning together
  • Spending What you spend each month or year, from your recent bank statements
  • Home Rent, or your mortgage balance, payment, and property value
  • Savings and assets Savings, investments, pensions, and any other property
  • Debts Loans, credit cards, and car finance

Estimates are fine. You can update anything as you talk.

    Before you start Live voice

    Live voice is optional. OpenAI processes your microphone audio for realtime transcription, conversation and the AI-generated voice you hear. You can always continue by typing.

    • Your completed spoken turns are processed automatically so the conversation can flow naturally.
    • Finalized transcripts and planning information are stored encrypted with your seven-day Planéir session. Planéir does not store raw audio or partial transcripts.

    • Planéir will summarise the plan before calculations. Only a clear answer to that summary can authorize the stated plan; calculations still run in Planéir's tested code.
