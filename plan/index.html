    Before your meeting begins

    A quick word before we start

    Your Planéir meeting is a live, hands-free conversation. Once you begin, your completed spoken answers are processed automatically so the conversation can flow naturally.

    • The live conversation can continue while you move between planning steps. Streaming stops when you end voice, withdraw consent, delete or leave the journey, hide the tab, lose the private session, or reach the server lease limit; Mute pauses microphone audio without ending the lease.
    • OpenAI Realtime interprets completed speech and calls protected tools, but its direct audio is blocked. A separate authenticated speech service reads only exact, code-owned Planéir copy.
    • Final spoken turns may invoke protected server-side tools that propose profile facts, follow-up questions, and relevant Planéir modules.
    • Proposals are not confirmed facts. Planéir reads back material amounts, dates, goals, and assumptions, then asks for one final profile-and-module confirmation.
    • Financial calculations still run only in tested Planéir code. The voice model cannot approve products or provide regulated advice.
    • Do not say PPS numbers, passwords, PINs, full account or card numbers, exact addresses, or identity-document details.
    A per-session meeting allowance applies. Each meeting is time-limited and covered by a server-enforced allowance.
