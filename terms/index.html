Using Planéir

These terms cover your private financial education workspace. The form currently runs as a local preview and saves a draft on this device only.

Who provides the service

Planéir is built and run by Gerry Boylan, a sole trader based in Dublin, Ireland. Contact hello@planeir.ie. These terms describe the private workspace; they do not replace the terms of an earlier invited session.

Financial education

Planéir provides financial education. Figures are illustrations based on the information and assumptions shown, not regulated advice or product recommendations.

The service is for adults aged 18 or over using their own household information. Share another person’s information only where you are entitled to do so and it is needed.

Information and assumptions

AI helps identify the scenarios to model from your objectives and organise the relevant modules. Financial calculations run in Planéir’s calculation engines. Read all the assumptions included with your results.

These results are generated automatically. Gerry does not routinely review individual workspaces. Modelled outcomes are not guarantees. Correct inaccurate information and obtain appropriate professional advice before making a financial decision.

Your saved workspace

The workspace is free. It remains until you delete it. The privacy notice explains processing, access and deletion.

Optional education call

After viewing your analysis, you can request a call with Gerry to talk through your figures, assumptions and modelled outcomes. The call details and booking terms appear there before you request it. Calls are private and unrecorded by default.

Availability and your rights

The app may be unavailable or produce an error. An incomplete analysis is identified; it is not a completed result. Nothing in these terms limits your mandatory rights under Irish or EU law.

Changes and contact

We explain material changes to the service or these terms before asking you to accept them. Irish law applies, subject to your mandatory consumer rights. Contact hello@planeir.ie about questions or complaints.
